# ISMIP7 Antarctica: meeting agenda, Friday 25 September 2026

Nine open issues on icepack/ismip7 bear discussing.

## Mesh and MAPs

### 1. Submission mesh and time step ([#20])

Choose between the Ua-style adaptive mesh (2 km at the grounding line and front, 180 km inland, 246,677 cells, dt 0.1 yr) and the 1000 m / 10 km gmsh pair `antarctica_10000_1000_buffered20000` (1.87 million vertices, dt 0.05 yr). Rice's MAPs are on a third mesh (2 km / 5 km gmsh) and reach the production mesh by transfer, and every MAP-check run from the 22 September snapshots stopped within 1.3 model years on both meshes it used, so drift and cost evidence waits on the final MAPs.

Still have problems at Amery. Persists in each mesh. Done with no buffer. Measures average velocity, Bedmachine thickness. Probably need to cut to 0.025. 73 years on 32 cores in 24 hours at fine resolution.

### 2. Chained inversion links change their objective ([#68])

Under `ISMIP7_LOG_VEL_WEIGHT=auto` each chained inversion link re-derives the log-velocity weight at its warm start, so the links of one MAP minimise different objectives (17,452 then 2,495 in IU's 32 km chain). Rice's 2 km chains for [#24] re-derived it the same way (Budd 2.10e6 then 1.96e5, RC 1.80e6 then 3.33e5).

Maybe already corrected?


## Initial state

The start year sets what the apparent mass-balance reference balances against, so these two are linked.

### 3. When the historicals and OCX start, and from which geometry ([#117])

Both historicals start from the 2015-dated MAP state placed at 1850, and OCX from the same state at 1979, so under the `balance` default the reference balances the 2015 geometry against 1850 forcing. A suggestion on the issue keeps the 2015 inversion and starts the historicals and OCX in a year between 1990 and 2010 (year open), from the 2015 state with its surface adjusted by the Smith et al. (2020) dH/dt and no re-inversion.

2003, subtracting the change map.

### 4. Keep the apparent mass-balance reference? ([#104])

The production runners default to `ISMIP7_APPARENT_MB=1` (`balance`), a frozen correction that zeroes the initial thickness tendency, and the group chooses `balance`, `div` or off. With it off at 32 km all 11 cores finished (5 with one rescued step) and drifted further, the controls gaining 864 to 874 mm SLE of VAF by 2301 against 360 to 378 with it; `div` and production resolution are untested.

Who knows?!?

## Forcing

Ocean melt, then surface mass balance, then ice-shelf collapse.

### 5. DG0 melt calibration ([#30])

Per-basin ΔT offsets are now fitted through the forward's own DG0 melt path ([PR 87]), and the refit against the July 1067.4 Gt/yr table fits every basin within ±2 K at the toolbox K05, K50 and K95 on both the 2 km and the 1000 m mesh. It closes once [#26] picks K and [#20] picks the mesh, since the offsets are specific to each mesh.

Do we need to do other values of K? Maybe need to ask somebody.


### 6. SMB-elevation feedback ([#116])

No run carries an SMB-elevation feedback; the SMB focus group's September README recommends the SMB gradient `dacabfdz` for Antarctica, and the protocol does not require one. The options are to submit without it (nothing reruns) or to add it from `dmrrodz` or `dacabfdz` (every core reruns, and most gradient files are still to be fetched).

Low priority. Kinda cool.


### 7. Ice-shelf collapse mode ([#10])

The submission names one of `none`, `mask` and `mask_front`, and the organisers leave the choice to each group (forum thread 30). IU's 32 km CESM2-WACCM ssp585 runs under all three modes are on the issue, and the two mask modes' VAF changes stay within about 7 mm SLE of each other through 2301; the corrected MRI-ESM2-0 v2 mask flags about 40 times the cells of v1 by 2250.

This is imposed calving with pre-defined rate.

## Output and README

### 8. Melt of ice gone by year end ([#109])

The `no_floating_ice` fill blanks `libmassbffl` wherever no floating ice is left at year end, so melt of ice gone within the year never reaches the gridded field: about 620 Gt/yr of real melt at 2300 in the 32 km ssp585, on top of the phantom melt of [#105]. The options are to state the amount in the README, to ask the organisers on the forum, or (option 3) to book the melt of grounded ice that goes afloat into an empty cell as front melt, `lifmassbf`, which is never filled.

Put it in the readme. Dump it in that variable.

### 9. README header fields ([#38])

The header needs names and choices from the group: contributors and affiliations, the submission date, the modeling group name and `source_id` (draft `RICE`), the model name and `ism_id` (draft `icepack2`), PPE and ESM participation, and the summary paragraph.
